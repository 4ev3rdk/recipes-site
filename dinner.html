Palak Paneer

Description

A creamy, green delight made with fresh spinach (palak) and soft paneer cubes. Palak Paneer is a rich, nutritious dish that's mildly spiced and pairs perfectly with roti, naan, or rice. It's a staple in North Indian households.

  • 2 cups spinach (palak) leaves, washed
  • 200g paneer, cut into cubes
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, pureed
  • 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 green chili (optional)
  • ½ tsp garam masala
  • ½ tsp cumin seeds
  • 2 tbsp cream or fresh malai
  • 2 tbsp oil or ghee
  • Salt – to taste

How to prepare

  1. Boil spinach leaves for 2–3 mins, then transfer to cold water. Blend into a smooth puree.
  2. In a pan, heat oil and add cumin seeds. Sauté onions till golden brown.
  3. Add ginger-garlic paste, sauté till raw smell disappears.
  4. Mix in tomato puree, green chili, and spices. Cook till oil separates.
  5. Add spinach puree and let it simmer for 4–5 minutes.
  6. Add paneer cubes and cream. Cook for 2–3 minutes and serve hot.
