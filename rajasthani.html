Gatte ki Sabzi

Description

Gatte ki Sabzi is a traditional Rajasthani dish made from gram flour (besan) dumplings cooked in a spiced yogurt-based gravy. It is rich, flavorful, and commonly served with roti or rice, making it a perfect meal for lunch or dinner.

  • 1 cup besan (gram flour)
  • 1/2 teaspoon red chili powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon garam masala
  • 1/2 teaspoon ajwain (carom seeds)
  • 1 tablespoon ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tablespoon curd (yogurt)
  • Salt to taste
  • Water as needed to form dough
  • Oil for frying
  • For the gravy: 1 cup yogurt
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 1 tomato, finely chopped
  • 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
  • 1 teaspoon coriander powder
  • 1 teaspoon cumin powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon garam masala
  • 1/2 teaspoon red chili powder
  • Salt to taste
  • Water as needed for gravy
  • Oil for tempering

How to prepare

Gatte (Gram Flour Dumplings)

  1. In a bowl, mix besan, red chili powder, turmeric powder, ajwain, salt, and ginger-garlic paste.
  2. Add curd and water gradually to make a stiff dough.
  3. Divide the dough into small portions and roll each portion into a cylindrical shape.
  4. Boil water in a large pan, add a pinch of salt, and drop the rolled dough into the boiling water.
  5. Boil for about 10–12 minutes or until the gatte rise to the surface.
  6. Remove them from the water and let them cool. Cut the gatte into round pieces and set aside.

Gravy

  1. In a pan, heat oil and sauté cumin seeds until they splutter.
  2. Add chopped onion and sauté until golden brown.
  3. Add chopped tomatoes, ginger-garlic paste, and cook until the tomatoes soften.
  4. Add coriander powder, cumin powder, red chili powder, and garam masala. Stir for a minute.
  5. Whisk yogurt with some water and add it to the pan. Cook for 5–7 minutes until the oil separates from the masala.
  6. Add water to adjust the consistency and let it simmer for 5 minutes.

Assemble

  1. Carefully add the gatte pieces to the gravy and cook for another 5 minutes.
  2. Serve hot with roti or rice.
