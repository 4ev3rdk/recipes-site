Masala Dosa

Description

Masala Dosa is a crispy, thin rice crepe filled with a spiced potato filling. It’s one of the most popular and iconic dishes of South India. Served with coconut chutney and tangy sambar, this savory dish is a perfect breakfast or dinner option.

  • 1 cup dosa rice
  • ½ cup urad dal (black gram)
  • 1 tablespoon fenugreek seeds (optional)
  • Salt - to taste
  • Oil or ghee for cooking
  • 2 large potatoes, boiled and mashed
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 1 green chili, chopped
  • ½ teaspoon mustard seeds
  • 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
  • A pinch of turmeric powder
  • Fresh coriander leaves, chopped
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice

How to prepare

Dosa Batter

  1. Soak dosa rice, urad dal, and fenugreek seeds in water for 4–6 hours.
  2. Grind the soaked ingredients into a smooth batter, adding water as needed.
  3. Allow the batter to ferment for 8–12 hours in a warm place.

Potato Filling

  1. Heat oil in a pan, add mustard seeds, cumin seeds, and chopped green chilies.
  2. Add onions and sauté until golden brown.
  3. Add turmeric powder and mashed potatoes. Mix well.
  4. Add salt, fresh coriander leaves, and lemon juice. Stir and cook for 2–3 minutes.
  5. Set aside the potato filling.

Making the Dosa

  1. Heat a non-stick or cast-iron pan on medium heat. Lightly grease with oil or ghee.
  2. Pour a ladleful of dosa batter onto the pan and spread it into a thin circle.
  3. Cook until the edges turn crispy and golden brown.
  4. Place a portion of the potato filling in the center and fold the dosa into a roll.
  5. Serve hot with coconut chutney and sambar.
