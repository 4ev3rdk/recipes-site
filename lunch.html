Chana Masala

Description

A hearty and flavorful North Indian dish made with chickpeas simmered in a spicy tomato-based gravy. Chana Masala is packed with protein and tastes best with rice or flatbreads like roti or naan. Perfect for lunch or dinner!

  • 1 cup dried chickpeas (or 2 cups canned)
  • 2 medium onions, finely chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, pureed
  • 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 tsp chana masala powder
  • ½ tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1-2 green chilies (optional)
  • Salt – to taste
  • Fresh coriander – for garnish
  • 2 tbsp oil

How to prepare

  1. Soak chickpeas overnight. Pressure cook until soft. If using canned, drain and rinse.
  2. Heat oil in a pan. Add cumin seeds and sauté onions till golden.
  3. Add ginger-garlic paste and sauté till raw smell goes.
  4. Add tomato puree, turmeric, and chana masala powder. Cook till oil separates.
  5. Add cooked chickpeas and 1 cup water. Simmer for 10–15 minutes.
  6. Garnish with chopped coriander and serve hot with rice or roti.

Nutrients - 1 Serving
