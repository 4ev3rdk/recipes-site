Kadhi Pakora

Description

Kadhi Pakora is a classic North Indian dish made with spiced yogurt-based gravy and fried gram flour dumplings (pakoras). The dish is rich, flavorful, and often served with steamed rice. It’s a comfort food that’s perfect for lunch or dinner.

  • 1 cup besan (gram flour)
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 1 green chili, chopped
  • 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 teaspoon red chili powder
  • 1 teaspoon ginger-garlic paste
  • Salt to taste
  • Oil for frying
  • For Kadhi: 2 cups yogurt
  • 1 tablespoon besan (gram flour)
  • 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
  • 1/2 teaspoon mustard seeds
  • 1-2 dried red chilies
  • 1/2 teaspoon fenugreek seeds (optional)
  • 1/2 teaspoon asafoetida (hing)
  • 1-2 tablespoons ginger, finely chopped
  • Salt to taste
  • Water as needed for gravy

How to prepare

Pakora (Gram Flour Dumplings)

  1. In a bowl, mix besan, chopped onion, green chili, red chili powder, turmeric powder, and salt.
  2. Add water little by little and mix well to form a thick batter.
  3. Heat oil in a pan and drop spoonfuls of batter to form small fritters (pakoras).
  4. Fry the pakoras until they turn golden brown and crispy.
  5. Remove and set aside on paper towels to remove excess oil.

Kadhi (Yogurt Gravy)

  1. In a bowl, whisk together yogurt, besan, turmeric powder, and salt.
  2. Add water to the mixture to make a smooth batter and set aside.
  3. In a pan, heat some oil and add cumin seeds, mustard seeds, dried red chilies, fenugreek seeds, and asafoetida.
  4. Once the spices begin to splutter, add chopped ginger and sauté for a minute.
  5. Pour in the yogurt mixture and bring to a boil, stirring constantly to avoid lumps.
  6. Once the kadhi comes to a boil, lower the heat and let it simmer for 10-15 minutes until the gravy thickens.

Assemble

  1. Place the fried pakoras into the kadhi and simmer for 5 minutes so the pakoras soak up the flavors of the gravy.
  2. Serve it
