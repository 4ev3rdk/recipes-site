Idli-Sambar

Description

Soft, fluffy idlis paired with hot, flavorful sambar make it a perfect breakfast or light meal. The idlis are steamed and healthy, while the sambar adds a spicy, tangy kick. Serve it with coconut chutney for an extra treat!

  • 1 cup idli rice (or parboiled rice)
  • ¼ cup urad dal (split black gram)
  • 2 tablespoons poha (flattened rice) - optional, makes batter softer
  • Water - as needed for soaking and grinding
  • Salt - to taste (add after fermentation)
  • ½ teaspoon fenugreek seeds (methi) - optional, helps fermentation

How to prepare

Idli

  1. Soak rice, urad dal, poha, and fenugreek seeds in water for 4–6 hours.
  2. Grind the soaked ingredients into a smooth batter using water as needed.
  3. Ferment the batter overnight or for 8–12 hours in a warm place.
  4. Add salt and mix the batter gently.
  5. Pour the batter into greased idli moulds and steam for 10–12 minutes.
  6. Remove and serve hot.

  3. Add chopped onions, tomatoes, and vegetables (like carrot, drumstick, etc.).
  4. Cook for a few minutes, then add tamarind water, salt, and sambar powder.
