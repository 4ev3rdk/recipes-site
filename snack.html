Onion Pakora

Description

Onion Pakoras are crispy, deep-fried Indian fritters made from sliced onions mixed with gram flour and spices. They're a perfect tea-time snack, especially on rainy days, and are loved for their crunch and flavor.

  • 2 large onions, thinly sliced
  • 1 cup besan (gram flour)
  • 2 tbsp rice flour (for extra crispiness)
  • 2 green chilies, finely chopped
  • 1 tsp ajwain (carom seeds)
  • A pinch of hing (asafoetida)
  • ½ tsp red chili powder
  • Salt – to taste
  • Water – as needed
  • Oil – for deep frying

How to prepare

  1. In a bowl, mix sliced onions with salt and let them rest for 10 minutes to release moisture.
  2. Add besan, rice flour, spices, and mix well. Add water little by little to form a thick batter.
  3. Heat oil in a deep pan. Drop small portions of the batter into hot oil.
  4. Fry on medium heat until golden brown and crisp.
  5. Remove and drain on paper towels. Serve hot with chutney or ketchup.
